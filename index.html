Welcome to the Anderson Family Business

It's 1994. Robert Anderson, 35 years old, is about to make the biggest decision of his life: quit his stable job and start his own manufacturing company.

His wife supports him, though she's nervous. They have three young children: Sarah (8), Michael (5), and Jennifer (2). The mortgage payment is due every month, and starting a business means uncertain income.

Over the next 50 years, you'll guide the Anderson family through multiple generations, critical business decisions, and family dynamics. Your choices will determine whether the company thrives or fails, and whether the family stays united or falls apart.

Remember: In family business, fair is not always equal, and equal is not always fair.
