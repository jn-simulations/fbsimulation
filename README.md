# Legacy & Leadership - Family Business Simulation

A 50-year interactive simulation game designed for MBA classes to teach family business dynamics, succession planning, and the challenges of balancing family relationships with business success.

## About the Game

Players guide the Anderson family from 1994 to 2044, making critical decisions about:
- Business growth vs. family stability
- Succession planning
- Fair vs. equal treatment of family members
- Crisis management (dot-com crash, 2008 recession, COVID-19)
- Exit opportunities (acquisition offers)
- Generational transitions

## Key Learning Objectives

- **Fair is not always equal** - Understanding different treatment of family members
- **Succession planning complexity** - When and how to transition leadership
- **Values vs. profits** - Balancing financial performance with family harmony
- **Long-term thinking** - 50-year decisions require strategic thinking
- **No perfect answers** - Every decision involves trade-offs

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` in a web browser
2. Read the introduction and click "Begin the Journey"
3. Make decisions at each event (every 2 years)
4. Watch how your choices affect both business metrics and family happiness
5. Complete the 50-year simulation to see your ending

## Files

- `index.html` - Main game interface
- `styles.css` - Visual styling
- `game.js` - Core game engine and state management
- `events.js` - All 21 decision events (1994-2044)
- `family.js` - Family member data and happiness tracking
- `endings.js` - Multiple ending scenarios based on your choices

## Scoring

The game evaluates you on two dimensions:

1. **Business Performance** (60% weight) - Revenue, profit, cash, debt management
2. **Family Harmony** (40% weight) - Average family happiness, family unity

## Educational Use

This simulation is ideal for:
- MBA family business courses
- Entrepreneurship classes
- Leadership and succession planning modules
- Ethics in business discussions

Created for teaching the complexities of family business management.
